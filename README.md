# Ecommerce-Sales-Analysis | PYTHON& SQL & POWERBI & EXCEL
## 📌 Project Overview

In today's competitive e-commerce landscape, organizations generate massive volumes of transactional data every day. However, transforming this raw data into meaningful business insights remains a significant challenge.

This project analyzes historical sales data to identify revenue drivers, profitability trends, customer purchasing behavior, discount effectiveness, regional performance, and product performance. The outcome is an interactive Power BI dashboard that enables business stakeholders to make informed, data-driven decisions.

# 🎯 Business Problem

An e-commerce company operates across multiple regions, customer segments, and product categories, processing thousands of customer orders over several years. Although sales continue to grow, management lacks visibility into critical business metrics such as:

* Profitability across products and regions
* Customer purchasing behavior
* Impact of discounts on revenue and profit
* Regional sales performance
* Product-level profitability
* Shipping performance and operational efficiency

Without these insights, strategic decisions regarding pricing, inventory, marketing, and customer retention become increasingly difficult.

The objective of this project is to transform historical sales data into actionable business intelligence through Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

* Technology products generated the highest overall sales.
* Consumer segment contributed the largest share of revenue.
* Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping mode.
* Certain products generated high sales but comparatively lower profit due to discounts.
* Higher discount levels significantly reduced overall profitability.
* Regional performance varied considerably, highlighting opportunities for targeted business strategies.
* Sales exhibited clear seasonal trends across different months.
* A small group of customers contributed a substantial portion of total revenue.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

* Optimize discount policies to improve profit margins.
* Increase marketing investment in high-performing customer segments.
* Focus inventory planning around top-performing product categories.
* Improve performance in underperforming regions through targeted campaigns.
* Promote high-profit products instead of only high-sales products.
* Develop customer loyalty programs to retain valuable customers.
* Continuously monitor shipping performance to enhance customer experience.
